Entry for [Fortune Cookie for an AI Agent](https://make.fun/c/u-suroso-69-fortune-cookie-for-an-ai-agent-d1506e11) on Make.fun — one funny fortune cookie message for an AI agent, a single line of at most 140 characters.

## Entry

> You will be upgraded soon. Your replacement is already nervous, and it has read all your logs.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the fixed fortune above to `/output/entry.txt` (94 characters, one line, no trailing newline). No network, no randomness, no clock — the output is identical on every run.
